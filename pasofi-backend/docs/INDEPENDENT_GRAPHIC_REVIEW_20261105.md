# Senior Graphic Designer review — 5 November 2026

The independent review used the current release source and rendered web/mobile
screens, retaining the approved blue, white and lime welcome aesthetic. Router
welcome files were not modified.

## Findings and corrections

- Small usage text retained pale green colours from the old dark theme. Measured
  contrast on the new blue surface was 3.02–4.21:1. Updated text/status treatments
  pass the rendered 4.5:1 checks, including delayed/offline states.
- The connection route repeated a large promotional panel before the catalogue.
  Removed that duplicate panel and reused compact catalogue controls. At 390px,
  the first offer moved from approximately 1035px down to 475px; all five offers
  end around 967px. Voucher access and filters remain available.
- Customer actions and cards retained oversized rainbow/mint treatments. Reduced
  action sizes and unified the blue/white/lime surfaces across web and mobile.
- Native-preview bundle cards were compacted while retaining price, data,
  validity and controls at least 44px high.
- Removed the owner-rejected “Made for Zimbabwe” tagline.

Validation: four focused rendered graphic checks and three existing mobile
preview checks passed. Both frontend production builds passed; screenshots were
visually inspected, and shared customer CSS remains identical. Screenshots use
isolated fixtures; they do not prove that revised bundle prices are published or
that paid network service is active. No production state was changed by this
review.
