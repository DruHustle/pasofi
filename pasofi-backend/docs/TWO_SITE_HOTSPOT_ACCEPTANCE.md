# Later physical hotspot acceptance

Use [the ordered cash hotspot launch runbook](CASH_HOTSPOT_LAUNCH.md) first. It connects the Mac application upgrade, separately protected RADIUS authority/ensure timer, private HTTPS reconciler, actual router/portal preparation and cash receipt/redemption to this physical acceptance. Every site's live evidence remains pending until observed; the prepared software is not a launch record.

Run only with the owner's authorization and a qualified private router/RADIUS transport. Identify each of the two MikroTik hAP lite devices, its RouterOS version, location, Starlink uplink, certificate and trusted management path. Record baselines and a recovery method before changes.

1. At each site, establish the intended Wi-Fi and captive welcome; verify unauthenticated users cannot reach unapproved destinations.
2. With a genuine controlled test account and voucher, verify redemption queues the correct allowance and validity begins only at the first confirmed connection. Repeat with the same account at the other site.
3. Attempt simultaneous connections at both sites and confirm the one-active-connection rule; verify device handoff and deliberate disconnect.
   For each session, observe final Stop, credential fencing, independent RouterOS absence and the ledger's closed state before reconnecting. A Stop alone retains the reservation. Show remaining allowance on same-site reconnect and then on the same account at the other site.
4. Compare measured upload and download totals with authoritative accounting at both routers. Verify exhaustion, validity and start-by expiry without granting extra access.
5. Interrupt the Starlink uplink, router and private tunnel independently; verify disconnect, conservative handling of uncertain accounting, and safe reconnection without duplicating allowances.
6. Attempt unauthorized NAS, invalid/expired/consumed voucher and unsupported authentication paths; verify rejection and absence of unintended access.
7. Record timestamps, device/site IDs, evidence and any exceptions without recording passwords, voucher codes or customer recovery credentials. Repeat on actual iOS and Android devices before qualifying native cookie behaviour.

Measure the intended simultaneous customer load and memory/uptime on the actual routers before assigning a capacity limit. Inspect and deliberately replace any old isolated pilot forwarding policy through a reviewed site-specific plan; never remove all pilot drops simply to obtain internet access.

An isolated lab, a sales-site record or a redeemed voucher is not evidence of physical connectivity. Keep network access and native cookie capabilities disabled until their separate acceptance is complete.
