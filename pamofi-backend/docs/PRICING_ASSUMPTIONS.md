# Pricing assumptions

The five candidate prices, decimal byte allowances and durations are a product proposal, not a claim of profitability or the lowest market price. The demo makes them visible; authorized administrators can now create immutable versions and publish reviewed offers; production defaults must remain unpublished until the operator approves the terms. The reported US$30/month Home/Residential Lite cost is unverified and cannot be assumed for both sites or treated as authorization for commercial use. Obtain site-specific plan, invoiced costs and permission before launch.

The owner has excluded Pesepay and selected a direct-provider/bank integration direction. Do not carry over an aggregator's published minimums or fees as the terms of a direct merchant contract. EcoCash, InnBucks and the selected acquiring bank must each confirm current currencies, minimum/maximum amounts, fixed and percentage fees, settlement and refund terms. See the dated [direct payment investigation](PAYMENT_INTEGRATION_RESEARCH_2026-11-02.md). Live digital checkout remains unavailable; the current sales path records exact cash confirmed by an authorized agent.

Published market tariff comparisons, tax, approved commissions and actual network capacity remain to be researched and dated before a pricing decision. For small bundles, model fixed payment costs separately from agent commission and bandwidth costs. A software test price is not evidence of commercial viability.
