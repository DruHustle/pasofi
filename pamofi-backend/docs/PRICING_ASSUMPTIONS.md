# Pricing assumptions

The five candidate prices, decimal byte allowances and durations are a product proposal, not a claim of profitability or the lowest market price. The demo makes them visible; production publication is not implemented and must default to draft. The reported US$30/month Home/Residential Lite cost is unverified and cannot be assumed for both sites or treated as authorization for commercial use. Obtain site-specific plan, invoiced costs and permission before launch.

On 1 November 2026, the [official Pesepay payment-method overview](https://developers.pesepay.com/payment-methods/overview/) listed USD EcoCash and InnBucks minimums of US$1. Merchant availability and current limits require verification; no provider method is enabled in this build. Published market tariff comparisons, fees, tax, commissions and actual capacity remain to be researched and dated before any pricing decision.
