# Pricing assumptions

The five candidate prices, decimal byte allowances and durations are a product proposal, not a claim of profitability or the lowest market price. The demo makes them visible; authorized administrators can now create immutable versions and publish reviewed offers; production defaults must remain unpublished until the operator approves the terms. The reported US$30/month Home/Residential Lite cost is unverified and cannot be assumed for both sites or treated as authorization for commercial use. Obtain site-specific plan, invoiced costs and permission before launch.

On 1 November 2026, the [official Pesepay payment-method overview](https://developers.pesepay.com/payment-methods/overview/) listed USD EcoCash and InnBucks minimums of US$1. Merchant availability and current limits require verification; real provider methods remain disabled by default and require explicit merchant/site qualification; the configured API filters current methods and amount limits before checkout. Published market tariff comparisons, fees, tax, commissions and actual capacity remain to be researched and dated before any pricing decision.
